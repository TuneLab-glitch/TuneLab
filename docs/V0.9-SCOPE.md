## Guidance evidence and applicability

Official sources reviewed 2026-10-02:

- [Hondata AFM tuning](https://www.hondata.com/help/flashpro/tuning_afm_flow.htm): warm varied driving and closed-loop trim review, with stock-starting-calibration prerequisites and voltage axes. These prerequisites are stated, not silently applied to modified hardware. TuneLab's Hz-based curve is a different workflow; voltage must not be mapped as Hz.
- [KTuner recording/saving](https://www.ktuner.com/KTunerHelp/tuning.htm): recording requires the device to be logging; saving preserves a datalog.
- [KTuner onboard logging](https://www.ktuner.com/KTunerHelp/on_board_logging.htm): supported hardware can record without PC intervention; actual hardware/configuration must be checked.

Boost/startup/validation capture plans are TuneLab guidance/inference, not an official vehicle-specific tuning procedure. Boost capture requires tuner-defined conditions on a dyno or closed course. No prescribed public-road gear/redline recipe is supplied. Additional channels are useful context but are not all analyzed by this viewer.
